# SWAT Plumbing — Blog Hero Image Prompts (Aug 28 – Oct 16 batch, 14 posts)

> **House style for blog heroes — derived from the published set in `public/blog/`.**
> These are NOT the GBP library style. Blog heroes are editorial object/scene
> photography:
>
> - **No people in frame.** No technicians, no hands, no homeowners.
> - **No branding.** No truck, no logo, no uniform, no shield overlay.
> - **16:9 landscape**, 1600px wide.
> - **Flat, natural light** — overcast daylight or soft interior daylight.
>   No dramatic lighting, no glow, no teal-orange grading, no lens flare.
>   The site's overlay component supplies the dark/tactical mood; the photo
>   stays clean and documentary.
> - **Real North Texas residential context** — brick and stone ranch homes,
>   Bermuda grass, live oaks, bare concrete, builder-grade interiors.
> - Subject reads clearly at a glance, with some negative space for the
>   headline overlay.
>
> **Pipeline:** generate PNG → drop in `public/blog/` → `node scripts/convert-blog-pngs.mjs`
> (resizes to 1600w, writes .webp at q82, deletes the PNG).

---

### 1. `water-heater-leaking-bottom-fort-worth.webp` — Aug 28

A thin film of water has spread across the bare concrete around the base and into a shallow black drip pan, with a faint rust-colored stain tracking from the brass drain valve. Beige painted wall behind, white baseboard. Soft natural daylight from camera-left, flat and even. Shot on Canon R5, 35mm, true-to-life color, fine grain. No people, no branding, no dramatic lighting.
