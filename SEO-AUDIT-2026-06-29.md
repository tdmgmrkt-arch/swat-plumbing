The foundation is genuinely good: rich, valid JSON-LD across page types; clean single-H1 structure; correct titles/meta/canonicals on the homepage, service leaves, and city pages; alt text on every image sampled; `llms.txt` live; real license number now rendered (`M-39596` — the `{{TBD}}` blocker is resolved); logo is now a WebP (the 1.2 MB SVG is gone). ## Findings (prioritized)

### HIGH

**1. Two blog posts canonicalize to a *different* page.** Self-canonical is correct on 6 of 8 posts, but:
- `/blog/24-hour-plumber-fort-worth-tx-fast-response/` → canonical = `/plumbing/emergency-plumbers/`
- `/blog/slab-leak-repair-fort-worth-tx-experts/` → canonical = `/plumbing/slab-leak/`

Both posts are in the sitemap with self-referencing `<loc>` entries, so the signals conflict. Google will consolidate these two posts into the service pages and drop them from the index. If that consolidation is intentional, they should be removed from the sitemap and ideally `noindex`'d; if not (likely a template/frontmatter bug pulling a "related service" URL into the canonical slot), point them at themselves.

**2. Double-branded, over-length titles on all 3 category hubs.**
`/plumbing/`, `/water-heater/`, `/water-quality/` all end with `| S.W.A.T. Plumbing | S.W.A.T. Plumbing LLC` (~85 chars → truncates in SERP, dilutes brand). Root cause is the known issue: the page's `metaTitle` appends `| S.W.A.T. Plumbing` *and* the root layout `title.template` appends `| S.W.A.T. Plumbing LLC`. Leaf, city, and blog pages are clean — only the hubs double up. Strip the brand from the three hub `metaTitle` values (this is the `metaTitle` lint rule already on the backlog).

### MEDIUM

**3. `og:image` missing on every non-homepage page.** Homepage has one; `/plumbing/`, service leaves, city pages, and `/contact-us/` emit zero `og:image` tags. Social/Slack/iMessage shares of those pages render no preview card. Set a default `openGraph.images` in root metadata (or per-template) so it propagates.

**4. Heavy HTML payload / unverified Core Web Vitals.** Homepage HTML is ~412 KB; hub/service pages 340–416 KB, largely from inline JSON-LD repeating the full 49-city `areaServed` + 31-item `OfferCatalog` on many page types. The schema itself is good — but consider scoping the full catalog to the homepage/hubs and trimming it on leaf/city pages. Also verify LCP/INP on the homepage's video hero (YouTube-style embed is a known LCP/INP risk). CWV was not field-measured in this pass — worth a Lighthouse/CrUX check before launch.

**5. Blog slug ↔ title mismatch.** `/blog/24-hour-plumber-fort-worth-tx-fast-response/` renders the title "What Counts as a Plumbing Emergency in Fort Worth?" — slug targets a different keyword than the content. Pick one so the URL, title, and topic align (also overlaps with finding #1 on this URL).

### LOW

**6.** Body copy shows `License #M-39596 (verify on TSBPE.gov)` — confirm the parenthetical is intended public-facing copy and not a leftover internal note.
**7.** Preview deployment is `index, follow`. Mitigated because canonicals point to prod, but `noindex` the Vercel preview alias before/after launch to keep the dev URL out of the index.
**8.** `robots.txt` `Sitemap:` already points to `www.swatplumbing.com` (correct for launch) — just confirm the prod domain is wired before go-live.
